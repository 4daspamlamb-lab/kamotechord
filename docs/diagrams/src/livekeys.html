With SHIFT held (top C) · moving around

  • Hold top C (SHIFT) and press:
  • G# / A# = scene ▲ / ▼ · F# = launch the selected scene
  • F / G = track ◀ / ▶ (keep holding: one more every 0.5 s); the 8 tracks shown follow
  • A = duplicate the selected clip into the next empty slot below · B = double its loop
  • E = fixed record length OFF → 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 bars
  • Clip Mode: hold low E (MOVE) instead and G# / A# move the selected clip up / down.

Fixed-length recording

  • Set it once: SHIFT + top E cycles OFF · 1 · 2 · 4 · 8 bars; the screen shows REC 4 BARS. Saved.
  • Record: KNOB BANK on an empty slot of an armed track records exactly that length, then the clip loops on its own; no stop press, never off the beat.
  • OFF = as now (KNOB BANK again stops).
  • Band, track by track: KNOB BANK → plays 4 bars, loops → top G# (next track, armed) → KNOB BANK …
  • Same keys in every mode, Clip Mode included.

Step Mode: hold a step + turn a knob

  • E1 velocity · E2 length · E3 repeats 1× / 2× / 3× · E4–E7 filter / delay / reverb / decay for that step only (automation: a later version)
  • No step held: knobs as everywhere; Fader 1 = velocity of new steps.
  • 16 drum keys (C2–D#3) = the 16 pads of a Drum Rack.

Record & overdub

  • Record a new clip: select an empty slot on an armed track (top G), press KNOB BANK. With a fixed length it stops by itself and loops; without, press KNOB BANK again.
  • Overdub: select a track whose clip is playing, arm it, press KNOB BANK; old notes stay. Again = stop. In Clip Mode: hold the lowest C + the clip's pad.
  • Capture: FADER BANK keeps what you just played. Undo: ◀. Tidy the timing: top F.
  • Arm several tracks: PATCH + top G adds a track instead of replacing.
